The Convention

One gathering, four nights, one expectation.

Every year the whole house comes together — every branch, every department, every generation — for four nights of worship, sound teaching and prayer. It is our reset button: we lay down the year so far, we listen, and we go back out carrying something we did not walk in with.

Bring your family. Bring the friend who has been meaning to come. There is no ticket, no levy and no reserved seating — only room prepared for you.
